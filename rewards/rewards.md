# Rewards: XP, Levels, Loot

## XP (permanent, never spent or lost)
| Size | Effort | XP |
|---|---|---|
| S | an evening | 10 |
| M | a few sessions | 25 |
| L | most of a week | 50 |
| XL | multi-week | 100 |

- Stretch goals: +25% of the issue's XP
- Rebuild-from-memory issues pay full XP

## Time Tokens
- 1 token = +30 min extra gaming, single use
- Max 7 redeemed per week
- Weekly goal met: +1 token
- Level-up: tokens equal to level number (L2 = 2, L3 = 3 ... capped at 7)
- Boss fight: +3 tokens
- Banked tokens expire after 8 weeks (TBD: product owner's call)

## Game unlocks (level-gated)
| Tier | Level | Unlock |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 4 | Kerbal Space Program (KSP1, PC) |
| 2 | 6-7 | Any game under $40 |
| 3 | 8-9 | Major unlock (TBD) |
| 4 | 10 | Legendary (TBD) |

## Guardrails
- XP is never deducted. If something slips, loot is paused until fixed.
- The product owner makes the final call on "done."
